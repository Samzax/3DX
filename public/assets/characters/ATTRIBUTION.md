# Player character assets

Everything in this folder is **CC0** (public domain dedication). Attribution is
not required; it is recorded here because provenance matters.

## Animation

`UAL1_Standard.glb` — "Universal Animation Library" by Quaternius (CC0). Only
its clips and the rig they were authored on are used; the body the clips play
on is rebuilt per character (`public/js/shared/mhbody.js`).

## Parametric body (`mh/`)

Generated by `node tools/build-character-kit.mjs --assets <extracted packs>`;
never hand-edit it.

- Base mesh (HM08), morph targets, the `game_engine` rig and its weights:
  **MakeHuman** (CC0), as shipped with **MPFB** (the MakeHuman plugin for Blender).
- Worn assets and skins: MakeHuman asset packs from
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html — only packs from
  the CC0 sections are used (`*_cc0.zip`). CC-BY packs must not be added here.
